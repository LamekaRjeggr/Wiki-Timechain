Add to the record

Write a kind-30828 card: start a timeline, add an entry to someone else's, or revise a card that is already there. Your key signs it, the relays keep it, and the viewer reads it on the next load — nothing here is stored on a server. How to write one · the spec.

1sign in

A card is a record. A record needs a real key behind it — no throwaway identity here. Three ways in, easiest first. Use the one you already have. The last two come back on their own next visit; the secret-key rung never does.

No key yet?

Skip the next one. Install a browser extension — nos2x or Alby — and let it make a key for you. Then come back to the rung it points at.

Secret key

Paste your secret key here if you are brave and have a stomach of steel… yeah, don't do that with your nostr key.

Or even better — use a throwaway key!

start here

Browser extension

An add-on in your browser holds your key. You click, it signs. Your key never touches this page. nos2x · Alby · Nostore · NIP-07

Remote signer

Your key lives on your phone or another device. Every signature asks that device first — you approve it there. Paste the bunker:// address your signer app shows you. NIP-46

or

Some signer apps — Primal among them — never show you an address. They take one instead. This page makes the invitation and waits for your app to answer.

Open this on the device holding your key and it should hand off to your signer app, which will ask you to approve. On another device, copy it across. Leave this tab open, and come back to it once you have approved — the answer arrives here, not there.
