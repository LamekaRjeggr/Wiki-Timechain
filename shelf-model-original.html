Blue = submitted (the author’s signature, always). Gold = accepted (a notary’s signature, only under passes: manual). Dim = passed once, not passing now. Dashed = deletion requested; a viewer hides it, relays may keep it. Three kinds: 30828 = a card, replaceable, the author’s current words · 30829 = a notary, replaceable, its passes setting and nothing else — no list · 8828 = one notary’s acceptance of one card, kept, holding a byte copy of what it read. A card passes upward only while a copy byte-equals the live words. An author’s revision changes the bytes; consent lapses; the card reads “revised ▸” until the notary accepts again. A curator cannot replace another author’s card — it publishes its own under its own key, same d, citing the source; that is a second submission of the same entry (“2 submitted”), accepted or not on its own. When the author’s words come to match a citing card byte for byte, the citing card is taken: the words agreeing is the whole evidence, no act; it holds no slot of its own. Drop = a revoke act on the acceptance; what was accepted stays readable in the copy.
